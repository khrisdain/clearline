# Cyber Incident Response Runbook

This runbook is for the Cyber Security Operations team. It is not for general distribution.

## Severity levels

Severity 1: confirmed compromise of Protected B data or an outage of a critical service such as 911 dispatch, water treatment control or payroll. Severity 2: confirmed compromise of a single workstation or account with no evidence of data access. Severity 3: suspicious activity under investigation.

## Escalation

A Severity 1 incident is escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer within 15 minutes and to the City Manager within one hour. The on-call incident commander opens a bridge line and starts the incident log. Severity 2 incidents are escalated to the CISO within four hours.

## Containment

Isolate affected hosts from the network using the endpoint protection console, preserve memory and disk images before rebuilding, and disable compromised accounts. Do not power off a host before memory is captured.

## External notification

Breaches involving personal information are reported to the provincial privacy commissioner as soon as feasible. The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security is notified of Severity 1 incidents through the city's liaison.
