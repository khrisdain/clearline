# Telework Policy

The City of Lakeview supports telework where the work allows it and service to residents is not affected.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may request telework. Front-line roles such as counter service, transit operations and field inspection are not eligible.

## Schedule

Eligible employees work on site at least three days a week and may telework up to two days a week. Telework days are agreed with the manager and recorded in the scheduling system. Teams may set common on-site days for meetings.

## Equipment and security

The city provides a laptop and a headset. Employees must connect through the city VPN, keep their screen out of sight of others, and must never print Protected information at home. A one-time home office allowance of $250 is available for a chair or desk.

## Ending an arrangement

Either the employee or the manager may end a telework arrangement with two weeks' written notice.
