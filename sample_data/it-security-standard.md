# IT Security Standard

This standard applies to every device and account connected to the City of Lakeview network.

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication

Network passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and every system that holds Protected information. Hardware security keys are issued to administrators.

## Patching

Critical security patches are applied to servers within 72 hours of release and to laptops within 7 days. High-severity patches are applied within 14 days. Systems that cannot be patched in time need an exception approved by the Chief Information Security Officer.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions are granted for a business need and use city-issued encrypted drives only.

## Reporting

Report lost devices and suspected phishing to the Service Desk at once, at extension 4357.
