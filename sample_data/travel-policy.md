# Travel and Expense Policy

City of Lakeview staff who travel on city business follow this policy. It applies to all employees, contractors billing travel to the city, and members of council.

## Booking travel

All air and rail travel must be booked through the city's travel desk at least 14 days before departure, except in an emergency approved by a director. Economy class is the standard for flights under six hours. Business class may be approved by a director for flights longer than six hours.

## Meals and incidentals

The daily meal allowance is $98 within Canada: $24 for breakfast, $26 for lunch and $48 for dinner. Incidental expenses are reimbursed at $17.30 per day. Meals provided by a conference or host are not claimed.

## Personal vehicles

Staff who use their own vehicle on city business are reimbursed at 68 cents per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Mileage is not paid for the normal commute to the employee's usual workplace.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims are submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of returning, with itemised receipts for every expense over $10. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.
