# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the City of Lakeview. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Your first day

Report to the People Services office on the second floor of City Hall at 9:00. You will receive your photo ID badge, laptop and the access card for your building. Bring two pieces of government-issued identification.

## Accounts and training

Your network account is created before your first day. You must complete the mandatory security awareness course and the privacy training within your first 10 working days; access to Protected systems is not granted until both are done.

## Probation

New permanent employees serve a six-month probation period. Your manager holds a check-in at three months and a review at six months.

## Benefits

Health and dental coverage begins on your first day. Enrol your dependants in the benefits portal within 31 days of your start date.
