# Procurement Guide

City of Lakeview staff who buy goods and services follow these thresholds and steps.

## Purchasing thresholds

Purchases under $10,000 can be made with a purchasing card or a single quote. Purchases from $10,000 to $100,000 need three written quotes. Purchases over $100,000 must go to an open public tender on the city's bidding site.

## Sole sourcing

Sole sourcing is allowed only when one supplier can meet the requirement, in an emergency, or for compatibility with existing equipment. It needs a written justification approved by the Purchasing Manager.

## Conflicts of interest

Staff involved in a purchase must declare any personal or financial relationship with a bidder and step back from the evaluation.
