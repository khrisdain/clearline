# 2027 Operating Budget Forecast

Draft for the Finance Committee. Figures are preliminary and confidential until council approval.

## Summary

The 2027 operating budget is forecast at $412.6 million, up 3.8 percent from 2026. The increase is driven by transit service expansion and collective agreement settlements.

## Property tax

Balancing the budget requires a residential property tax increase of 2.9 percent, which adds about $118 a year to the average home.

## Pressures

Transit operating costs rise by $9.4 million with the new Route 22 express service. Winter maintenance is budgeted at $14.1 million, based on the five-year average snowfall. Insurance premiums rise 11 percent.

## Reserves

The forecast draws $3.2 million from the tax stabilisation reserve, leaving a reserve balance of $21.7 million at the end of 2027.
